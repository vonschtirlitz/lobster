# antislop audit 001: LOBSTER

Date: 2026-10-27
Mode: after (session override). From here on this session works in "during" mode.
Scope: the app UI (`index.html`, `src/ui/`), UI copy (including the drill module's words of command), `README.md`, code comments, `Makefile`, `terraform/`, `.github/workflows/`, and `docs/superpowers/`.
Skills applied: core, ui, human, layoutmobile, copywriting, code.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.
Nothing has been changed. Pick numbers to fix; numbers not picked stay as they are.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-37: no written design direction.** There is no `DESIGN.md`, no Design Read and no declared ENERGY / RHYTHM / MOTION dials. The current palette (parchment field, oxblood accent, EB Garamond for headings and words of command) was chosen to suit the 18th-century subject, but those reasons are not written down. Under R-37 the UI is a "draft without direction" until you supply direction or approve one.
2. **R-02: em dashes in UI text.**
   - Every word of command in `src/drill/gb1764.ts` uses `—` ("To the Right — Wheel — Halt, Dress", "March — 20 paces — Halt", "Rear ranks — take open order — March", "Form 3 deep — March", "To the Right about — Face"). These are shown in the orders list and the replay bar.
   - The `Makefile` help line also uses one ("LOBSTER — available targets:").
   - Period manuals do print a dash between the cautionary and executive parts of a command, so this collides with R-02 and needs your call: keep the period dash as a deliberate override, or swap it for another separator (comma, colon, or a line break between caution and execution).
3. **R-02: em dashes in comments and docs.**
   - `terraform/main.tf:69` ("OAC — lets CloudFront…", copied from the material-culture stack).
   - 11 places in `docs/superpowers/` (spec and plan), all quoting words of command.
4. **R-38 / R-23: placeholder drill values shown as if final.** Every 1764 number and word of command is a placeholder, marked `SOURCE NEEDED` in code and noted in the README. The app itself shows them with no hint, and the drill selector just says "1764 Manual", so a user rehearsing real calls would take them as sourced.
5. **R-25 (non-text contrast): platoon shading fails on the field.** Platoons after the first are tinted toward white. Against the field (#e7e3d4) the 2nd platoon's shade measures about 3.1:1, the 3rd about 2.0:1 and the 4th about 1.3:1, all for the default red and blue. Men in the 3rd and later platoons are close to invisible. Soldiers are interactive marks and need 3:1.
6. **R-34 / R-25: the dark theme fails on the canvas.**
   - The app follows the system dark mode, and the panels pass there (muted 5.8:1, error 5.7:1, warning 9.0:1).
   - The company colours on the dark field measure between 1.7:1 and 2.6:1 (default red 2.4:1, blue 2.2:1, purple 1.7:1), so the men disappear.
   - Dark mode was never checked visually.
7. **R-32: field editing is mouse-only.** Moving, rotating, frontage and the edge handles only work with a pointer. A keyboard user can select a company in the list but can't place it or its ghost.
8. **R-32 / R-26: some controls can't be used from the keyboard.**
   - Each order's words are a `role="button"` with `tabindex=0` that responds only to click, so Enter and Space do nothing (`src/ui/panels/orders.ts`).
   - Reordering orders is drag-only, with no move up or down control.
9. **R-26: "Suggest orders" is a dead control.** It's a disabled button whose only explanation is a hover tooltip. Keyboard and touch users never see why it's there. R-26 allows a placeholder only with a visible "Coming soon" label and a `// TODO`.
10. **R-27: the field has no empty state.** With every company removed, the canvas is a blank grid with nothing on it. The side panel says "Select a company", but nothing on the field says how to start.
11. **R-03: there is no mobile or narrow layout.**
    - The page is a fixed two-column grid (`1fr 360px`) with no breakpoints, so it overflows sideways on a phone or narrow window.
    - Buttons and inputs are 30px tall (minimum 44px).
    - The design made mobile a non-goal ("desktop with a mouse is the target"), which collides with R-03, a Hard Gate. Your call: keep desktop-only as a recorded override, or add a narrow layout.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

12. **R-06: uppercase labels with wide tracking.** The side panel's subheadings ("1ST COMPANY", "OFFICERS", "NCOS AND DRUMMER", "FORMATION (EDITING GHOST)") are uppercase with 0.06em tracking, a default label style with no written reason. Sentence-case headings in the heading face would carry the same hierarchy.
13. **Non-text contrast: input and control borders are too faint.** The border colour #d9d4c4 measures 1.4:1 against the panel, so text inputs, number fields and selects barely show their edges (WCAG 1.4.11 asks for 3:1).

## LOW (Quality Locks / comments)

14. **R-31 (comments): decorative banner separators.** `terraform/*.tf` has `# ── S3 bucket ────…`-style banners. These copy the material-culture repo's house style. Keep them for consistency with that repo, or turn them into plain one-line comments.
15. **Comments that restate or only label code.**
    - `src/ui/canvas/render.ts` has `// ghost`, `// selection`, `// the company itself` and `// gap line or ground arrow` as block labels.
    - `camera.ts` has `/** Drag the ground by a screen delta. */` above `pan()`, and `render.ts` has `/** Where the rotate handle sits for a formation. */` above `rotateHandlePos()`. Each restates its name.
16. **Over-long comments.** These blocks run 3–4 lines where one would do:
    - the header comments of `terraform/main.tf`, `cloudfront.tf` and `github-oidc.tf`;
    - the `environment` note in `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`.
17. **The drill selector is a one-option control with a no-op change handler** (`src/ui/panels/setup.ts`). It reads as a working control that does nothing. Show the drill system as text until a second manual exists, or keep the select with a "More manuals coming" note.

## Checked and passing

- **Copy:**
  - No AI buzzwords, fabricated statistics, testimonials or claims in the UI or README.
  - CTAs are specific: "Make this the start", "+ Wheel", "Add company".
  - No decorative emoji in the UI. The ✓ and ✗ in the ghost readout come with text.
- **Panel contrast:** light-mode text passes (muted 5.4:1 and 5.0:1, error 6.3:1, warning 12.3:1, selection 5.0:1). Dark-mode panel text passes too.
- **Keyboard and states:**
  - Focus styles are present and `outline` is never removed.
  - The company list works from the keyboard (Enter and Space).
  - Error states are text first: invalid orders give a reason, a bad file shows a dismissible alert, and unreached orders say "Not reached".
- **Honest placeholders and no slop patterns:**
  - The logo and favicon were requested explicitly.
  - Every placeholder value is marked `SOURCE NEEDED` in code.
  - No gradients, glow, glassmorphism or background grids. The grid on the field is a measurement grid in paces, with the scale bar.
